AI Photo Series / 2026

Gravity Is Optional

An experimental AI photo series about the impossible intruding into everyday life in English social housing.

This project started with a simple question. Can generative AI create something that feels like documentary observation instead of polished AI imagery.

Instead of chasing cinematic perfection, the series looks for awkward framing, harsh flash, clutter and proximity. The impossible appears inside ordinary domestic scenes.

The visual reference was Richard Billingham. His photographs of everyday life in English social housing informed both the documentary distance and the refusal of polish.

Context

Social housing, suspended reality.

The project tests whether generative image systems can imitate a visual culture that was never meant to be spectacular.

Many AI images look polished and cinematic. This series wanted the opposite: images that feel raw, uncomfortable and strangely real.

The ordinary is not replaced by spectacle. It is interrupted. Small visual impossibilities appear inside rooms, stairwells and housing blocks that still read as everyday environments.

The result is less a fantasy world than a pressure on documentary realism. The image keeps its social texture while something physically impossible slips into view.

Process

Controlled imperfection.

The challenge was not inventing the idea. The challenge was forcing the image system away from slick defaults and into a rougher photographic language.

Reference analysis

Recurring elements from documentary photography shaped the prompts and the selection.

  • small rooms
  • direct flash lighting
  • unplanned framing
  • visible clutter

Visual structure

The series moves from ordinary environments toward stronger disturbances without losing the mundane tone.

  • ordinary environment
  • small visual disturbances
  • surreal final image

Prompt strategy

Prompts avoided cinematic language and instead described documentary qualities directly.

  • documentary camera
  • harsh flash lighting
  • grainy texture
  • awkward framing
Series

Floating lives.

The image sequence is shown as a quiet visual index. The series keeps the documentary register while letting gravity, objects and bodies drift slightly out of order.

Domestic spaces, residents, fragments of life in almost empty rooms and surreal moments blend documentary realism with the impossible.

Reflection

Documentary in the age of AI.

The project became a study of visual control: how far a photographic language can be reconstructed through prompting, selection and refusal.

Instead of producing visually impressive AI imagery, the series experiments with documentary aesthetics and their social tone.

The important question is not whether the impossible can be rendered, but whether it can appear inside a world that still feels observed rather than staged.

That tension between precision and friction is what carries the series.
